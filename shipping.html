WebCraftly operates as a curated digital marketplace delivering digital tools, production-grade workbooks, spreadsheets, developer codebases, and creative assets. All orders are processed using an automated digital delivery engine.

Electronic Delivery Schedule:
  • Processing Time: Automated instantly upon successful payment authorization (0 to 5 minutes).
  • Immediate Screen Access: Once your transaction verifies, you are instantly redirected to the download screen with active temporary signed download tokens.
  • Email Notification & Backup Link: A formal purchase confirmation, invoice receipt, and direct access link are automatically dispatched to the email provided at checkout.
  • My Library Dashboard: If you are logged in to WebCraftly, purchased digital assets are permanently linked to your verified account and accessible anytime under My Library.

Physical Merchandise Notice: In the event that a creator offers a physical companion (e.g. printed spiral-bound workbooks or physical development kits), order handling and warehouse processing requires 1 to 2 business days, followed by standard carrier transit of 3 to 5 business days for domestic addresses.

WebCraftly serves a global community of builders, developers, and knowledge workers. Our electronic infrastructure complies with international e-commerce delivery and financial gateway standards:

payments International Payment Gateway Regulations (Razorpay)

Transactions are routed through our authorized banking partner, Razorpay. Indian domestic orders support UPI, NetBanking, and RuPay cards (denominated in INR ₹). International transactions support Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Apple Pay (denominated in USD $). Currency conversion rates and any nominal foreign transaction fees are determined transparently by your issuing financial institution.

Because digital products are delivered over the internet, they are exempt from standard physical freight customs tariffs and import clearance delays. However, customers are responsible for any statutory local digital service taxes (such as GST in India, VAT in the European Union / United Kingdom, or applicable state sales taxes in the US) that are mandatory under applicable tax laws.

We want you to be completely satisfied with the craft and utility of your digital purchases. If you encounter issues with a purchased digital asset, we provide a structured exchange and resolution process:

Eligibility Window: Exchange requests must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the original order timestamp.
Step-by-Step Exchange Procedure:
  1. Initiate Request: Email our support desk at contact@webcraftly.site or submit a ticket through our Contact Portal.
  2. Provide Order Details: Specify your registered checkout email, Razorpay Payment ID (e.g. pay_...), and the title of the digital product.
  3. State the Exchange Reason: Describe the technical difficulty (e.g. corrupted PDF archive, incompatible version, or accidental purchase of an alternate tool bundle).
  4. Rapid Resolution: Our engineering team reviews the request and dispatches a verified, corrected archive, updated license token, or an exchange voucher of equal value within 24 business hours.
Condition Requirements for Replacement:

The product must demonstrate a verified technical flaw, missing module, or incompatibility with advertised environment specifications. For physical companions (if applicable), the item must be unused, in its original packaging, and free from user-inflicted wear.

When an exchange cannot satisfy the customer's request and a refund is approved under our 7-Day Risk-Free Guarantee, funds are returned directly to the original payment source:

Approval & Gateway Reversal
Approved refunds are submitted immediately into Razorpay within 24 hours of claim verification. An official gateway confirmation email with refund ARN/RRN tracking is sent.
Bank Settlement Cycles
Depending on your card issuer or banking network, the credit will appear on your account within 5 to 7 business days. Domestic UPI reversals typically settle within 24 to 48 hours.
