Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 29, 2026

01 Introduction & Scope

Welcome to WebCraftly (accessible at https://www.webcraftly.site). WebCraftly operates a curated global marketplace and collective for premium digital assets, developer tools, spreadsheet models, and educational resources.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personally identifiable information through transparent practices, strict cryptographic controls, and compliance with global privacy regulations including GDPR and applicable consumer protection statutes.

02 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to facilitate asset delivery, licensing, and transactions:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, email address, and authentication identifiers when you register or communicate with our support desk.
  • Transactional Data: Purchase history, license activation keys, and receipt identifiers. We do not store credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP address (anonymized for region and fraud detection), browser type, and interaction metrics to optimize performance.

03 Payment Processing & Third-Party Partners

Payment transactions on WebCraftly are processed securely through accredited payment gateway partners, including Razorpay.

When completing checkout, your payment payload is transmitted directly to the gateway via 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption conforming to the highest PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance standards. Razorpay manages tokenization, fraud monitoring, and automated currency conversions on our behalf.

04 How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Deliver instant digital download links and automated receipt emails.
  • Issue and verify immutable lifetime personal or commercial license certificates.
  • Detect and mitigate payment fraud, unauthorized asset resale, and abuse.
  • Deliver critical security notices, product updates, or support ticket responses.

We will never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers.

05 Cookies & Local Storage

We use functional cookies and browser local storage strictly to remember your active checkout session, country preference, theme settings, and saved wishlist items. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although certain cart functions may be affected.

06 Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal account data. To submit a data inquiry or erasure request, contact our Data Protection Officer at contact@webcraftly.site. We honor verified requests within 30 business days.
